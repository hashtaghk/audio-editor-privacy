Privacy Policy

Audio Editor for Android · package harix.ringtone.maker.music.editor.free
Effective 18 August 2026 · Last updated 19 August 2026

The short version. Audio Editor edits your files entirely on your device — your audio and video never leave your phone and are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. The app has no accounts and no sign-in. The one thing that does reach outside your device is advertising: we show ads through Google AdMob, and AdMob collects device-level data to select and measure those ads.

This policy explains what Audio Editor (“the app”, “we”, “us”) collects, why, and what control you have. It applies to the Android app published by Harix DevCo and to nothing else.

1. Who we are

Audio Editor is developed and published by Harix DevCo. For any privacy question or request, contact us at support@hashtaghk.com. We are the data controller for the limited processing described below; Google acts as an independent controller for the advertising data it collects.

2. What stays on your device

All editing — trimming, merging, mixing, format conversion, speed and pitch changes, echo and equalizer effects, ringtone creation and audio-to-video conversion — runs locally using an FFmpeg library bundled inside the app. Specifically:

  • Your audio and video files are never uploaded to our servers or to any third party.
  • We do not read, index, or transmit the contents, filenames, or metadata of your media.
  • The app does not browse your video gallery. When you convert a video to audio, Android’s own photo picker asks you to choose one file, and the app receives access to that file alone.
  • Edited files are written to your device’s own storage (by default the Download/AudioEditor folder) and stay under your control.
  • App settings such as your chosen language, theme colour and dark-mode preference are stored only in local app preferences on your device.

We operate no server, no account system, and no user database. Uninstalling the app removes its local data from your device.

3. Advertising

The app is free and is funded by advertising. We use Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) to serve banner, native, interstitial and rewarded interstitial ads.

What AdMob collects

To select, deliver, cap, measure and protect ads against fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes information automatically, including:

  • Your Android Advertising ID (AAID) — a resettable, non-permanent identifier;
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate (city or region level) location may be derived;
  • Device and app information — device model, operating-system version, language, screen characteristics, network type, and the app’s package name and version;
  • Ad interaction data — which ads were requested, shown, viewed or clicked, and when.

This data is collected by Google under its own privacy terms, not by us. We never receive your Advertising ID or your IP address; we only see aggregated, anonymous performance reports in the AdMob console. We do not sell personal information, and we do not combine advertising data with anything else.

4. Consent and personalised ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a Google-certified consent message the first time you open it, using Google’s User Messaging Platform. Through that message you choose whether your data may be used for personalised ads or only for non-personalised ads.

  • Personalised ads are chosen using data about your interests and prior activity.
  • Non-personalised ads are based only on general context such as the app’s content and your coarse location. Google still uses limited data for frequency capping, aggregate ad reporting and fraud prevention, which is necessary for the service to work at all.

Your choice is recorded on your device and honoured on every subsequent request. You can change or withdraw it at any time — see the next section. If you are outside these regions, the consent message is not shown and the controls under “Your choices” still apply.

5. Your choices

You are not required to accept personalised advertising to use any feature of the app.

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID — open Android Settings › Privacy › Ads (the exact path varies by manufacturer). You can reset the identifier, which disconnects it from past activity, or delete it entirely, which stops apps from receiving it at all.
  • Opt out of ads personalisation — in the same Android settings screen, or through Google Ad Settings.
  • Change your consent choice — open Settings › Privacy Options inside the app to reopen the consent message and update or withdraw your choice at any time. This entry appears wherever the consent message applies.
  • Deny permissions — you may refuse any runtime permission. Features that depend on it will be unavailable, but the rest of the app continues to work.
  • Uninstall — removing the app stops all collection and deletes its local data.

7. Analytics and crash reporting

The app does not include an analytics or crash-reporting SDK. We do not track feature usage, build behavioural profiles, or receive crash reports containing your data. Aggregate install and crash statistics may still be shown to us by Google Play; those are provided by Google under its own privacy policy and are not linked to you by us.

8. Children

Audio Editor is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad content is filtered through AdMob’s content-rating controls. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at support@hashtaghk.com and we will act on it promptly.

9. Data retention, security and transfers

Because we run no servers, we hold no personal data to retain or delete. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained and secured according to Google’s data retention practices, and may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in Google’s privacy documentation. Communication between the app and Google’s ad servers is encrypted in transit.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Residents of California may additionally have the right to know what personal information is collected and to opt out of its sale or sharing — note that we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA.

Because the advertising data described in section 3 is held by Google rather than by us, requests concerning it are best directed to Google, or exercised through the device controls in section 5. For anything within our control, write to support@hashtaghk.com and we will respond within 30 days.

11. Third-party components

The app bundles open-source libraries that run entirely on your device and send no data anywhere, including FFmpeg (audio and video processing), ExoPlayer (playback), Glide (image loading) and Lottie (animations). The only component that communicates over the network is the Google Mobile Ads SDK, together with Google’s User Messaging Platform used for the consent message.

The “Last updated” date at the top always reflects the current version, and material changes will be highlighted in the app’s Play Store release notes. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
